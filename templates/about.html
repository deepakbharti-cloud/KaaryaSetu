About KaaryaSetu

Find a trusted domestic helper in your area

KaaryaSetu ek simple platform hai jahan customers apne area ke skilled workers ko easily find, compare aur book kar sakte hain.

KaaryaSetu kya hai?

KaaryaSetu ka purpose customers aur skilled workers ke beech ek easy aur trusted connection banana hai. Agar kisi customer ko plumber, electrician, carpenter, cleaner ya kisi aur skilled worker ki zarurat hai, to woh apne area ke available workers ko easily search kar sakta hai.

Customer worker ki profile, skills, experience, rating aur charges dekhkar booking request bhej sakta hai. Worker request ko accept ya reject kar sakta hai aur booking complete hone ke baad customer rating aur review bhi de sakta hai.
